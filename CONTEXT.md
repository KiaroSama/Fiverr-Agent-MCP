# Fiverr Operations

A local workspace for a Fiverr account owner to inspect permitted records, prepare work and control account actions. A local result and a platform outcome are distinct.

## Language

**Account**: The particular Fiverr identity and seller/buyer roles whose records an owner is entitled to access.
_Avoid_: Browser title, merely logged-in page.

**Gig**: A Fiverr seller's listed service with category-specific packages, requirements and publication state.
_Avoid_: Order, offer, generic job.

**Order**: A specific buyer-seller engagement whose state, deliverables, deadlines and contractual terms belong to its participants.
_Avoid_: Gig, local task.

**Custom offer**: Terms proposed to a particular buyer/conversation, distinct from an accepted order.
_Avoid_: Purchase, accepted contract.

**Mira opportunity**: An account-visible Fiverr Inbox introduction associated with Mira matching.
_Avoid_: Universal buyer directory, public job board, new Brief.

**Historical Brief**: An older Brief record, explicitly distinct from a current Mira opportunity.
_Avoid_: Current universal project feed.

**Draft**: Saved local content that has not been sent, uploaded or published to Fiverr.
_Avoid_: Submitted message, live gig, delivered order.

**Prepared action**: An immutable scoped proposal for a specific account/entity, pending human approval and state revalidation.
_Avoid_: Executed action, confirmation flag.

**Human approval**: A server-verified, expiring, single-use owner decision on one exact action.
_Avoid_: Model confirmation, tool argument consent.

**Unknown outcome**: A dispatched action whose platform result has not been established; replay is unsafe until reconciliation.
_Avoid_: Failed send, automatic retry.

**Manual handoff**: A documented next step that the owner must complete in the official interface; it is not a completed platform action.
_Avoid_: Remote success, implemented automation.

**Observation**: A source-derived record with provenance and an observation time, not an invented estimate or verified-current account state.
_Avoid_: Live truth, guaranteed rank.
